# CAPS curriculum map

PhysicsMzansi covers CAPS Physical Sciences for Grades 10, 11 and 12, across six knowledge strands. Paper 1 covers **Mechanics**, **Waves, Sound & Light** and **Electricity & Magnetism**, plus the photoelectric effect in Grade 12. Paper 2 covers **Matter & Materials**, **Chemical Change** and **Chemical Systems**.

## How the papers are built

These are the approximate marks per strand. Always check the latest DBE examination guidelines.

| Paper | Grade 10 (2 h) | Grade 11 (3 h) | Grade 12 (3 h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Physics: Mechanics** | 75 | 68 | 63 (incl. Gr 11 Newton's laws) |
| **P1: Waves, sound & light** | 40 | 32 | 17 |
| **P1: Electricity & magnetism** | 35 | 50 | 55 (incl. Gr 11 electrostatics) |
| **P1: Matter (photoelectric effect)** | – | – | 15 |
| **P2 Chemistry: Matter & materials** | 75 | 58 | 42 (organic chemistry) |
| **P2: Chemical change** | 60 | 80 | 90 |
| **P2: Chemical systems** | 15 | 12 | 18 (fertilisers) |

Both papers are out of 150 marks. NSC papers come with **data sheets**: physical constants and formulae for Paper 1; the periodic table and the table of standard reduction potentials for Paper 2. These are in the Toolkit and in *Data sheets* in this guide.
