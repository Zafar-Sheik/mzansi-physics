# Overview

**PhysicsMzansi** is an interactive Grade 10, 11 & 12 Physical Sciences companion for South African learners. It follows the **CAPS** curriculum and the **NSC** exam format: **Paper 1 (Physics)** and **Paper 2 (Chemistry)**. The syllabus comes in six strands:
- **Mechanics**
- **Waves, Sound & Light**
- **Electricity & Magnetism**
- **Matter & Materials**
- **Chemical Change**
- **Chemical Systems**

It is the sister app of **MathsMzansi**, **CodeMzansi** (IT) and **GeoMzansi** (Geography). It shares their learning design and accessibility features, and the same promise: no accounts and no tracking.

## What's inside

- **48 bite-sized lessons** covering all 45 CAPS topics across the three grades. Each lesson is split into steps of 3–10 minutes:
  - a real-world hook
  - a live simulation
  - worked examples revealed one step at a time
  - typed-answer practice
  - a quick quiz
- **39 simulations and labs.** Nothing ever moves until the learner presses **Play**, and every lab has an *Explain* box. Physics labs:
  - motion graphs, an energy skate park, vectors, forces on a slope, gravitation
  - collisions, projectiles, work and energy with friction
  - pulses, waves, sound, the EM spectrum, refraction and total internal reflection (TIR), diffraction, the Doppler effect
  - charge sharing, magnetic fields, circuits, Coulomb's law and fields, Faraday's law, internal resistance, generators and motors
  - the photoelectric effect and atomic spectra

  Chemistry labs:
  - states of matter, an atom builder, an interactive periodic table, VSEPR shapes, boiling-point charts
  - a gas-laws lab, an organic-molecule builder, an equation balancer, a mole calculator
  - energy profiles, collision theory, Le Chatelier's principle, a virtual titration, galvanic and electrolytic cells
  - the NSC data sheets
- **Endless practice.** 43 question generators, from the equations of motion to cell emf and NPK ratios. Every question is new, is checked instantly and comes with a full worked solution.
- **Smart answer checking.** Learners type real answers such as `12 m/s`, `v = 1,5 m·s⁻²`, `3 × 10^8`, `6,4e-19 J`, `CH3COOH` or `propan-1-ol`. The checker understands units, decimal commas and scientific notation. Its feedback is specific: "check your sign", "check your unit prefix (×1 000)", "did you forget to multiply by g?".
- **Flashcards** with spaced repetition for 349 key terms and laws, plus a searchable glossary.
- **Quiz Arena**: mixed-topic rounds, relaxed or timed.
- **Exam Room**: 95 NSC-style questions (299 parts) with marking memos, one tick per mark, covering every topic.
- **Mock papers**: timed Paper 1 and Paper 2 mocks for each grade. They are self-marked and give an NSC achievement level and a breakdown by topic.
- **Weak-spot coach**: per-topic mastery built from everything a learner does, with the next lesson and a targeted drill.
- **Worksheet generator** for teachers: printable worksheets with memos.
- **Games**: Speed Solve, Element Quest, Formula Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out and Science Hangman.
- **Progress**: XP, levels, streaks, badges, a 12-week activity map, and export/import.

## Who it's for

- **Learners** revising at home, catching up on a topic they missed, or doing the "practical" when the school has no lab.
- **Teachers** projecting the simulations in class (the Toolkit page), setting a lesson as homework, or using the Exam Room for self-marked practice.
- **Every brain.** It's designed with ADHD, dyslexia and other learning differences in mind (see *ADHD, dyslexia & accessibility*).

> PhysicsMzansi is an independent study aid. It is not a substitute for real practical work and lab safety training. Always check exam details with your teacher and the latest DBE examination guidelines.
