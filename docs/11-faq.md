# FAQ & troubleshooting

**My progress disappeared.** Progress lives in the browser. Clearing browsing data, using a private/incognito window, or switching browser or device starts fresh. Use *Export progress* on the Progress page to back it up.

**The checker says my answer is wrong, but it's right!**
- Check the unit: if the question asks for metres, `150 cm` won't match `1,5`.
- Check the sign: in Physics a negative sign usually means a direction.
- Don't round too early.
- You can write `3 × 10^8`, `3e8` or `300 000 000`.

If it's still marked wrong, the "Show me how" button reveals the memo. Please tell your teacher so the question can be fixed.

**Do I have to type the unit?** No. If you do, it's checked and understood, including prefixes like k, m, μ and n.

**Which value of g is used?** 9,8 m·s⁻², as on the NSC data sheet.

**Is Grade 10 included?** Yes. All three FET grades are covered, with 45 CAPS topics across Paper 1 (Physics) and Paper 2 (Chemistry).

**Can it replace practical work?** No. The simulations help learners understand and prepare for practicals, but CAPS requires hands-on practical work and formal assessment tasks at school.

**Animations make me dizzy.** Turn on *Calm mode* in Preferences, or your device's "reduce motion" setting. Simulations only move when you press Play.

**Can I use it offline?** Once a page has loaded it keeps working, but there is no full offline mode yet.
